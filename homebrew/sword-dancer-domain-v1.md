# Sword Dancer Domain
*Cleric Subclass for D&D 5.5e / 2024 Rules — Version 1*

Sword Dancers practice a sacred tradition combining swordplay, song, dance, moonlight, and divine magic. Most closely associated with **Eilistraee, the Dark Maiden**, Sword Dancers transform combat into both prayer and performance.

Their blades sing heroic hymns, dance independently through the air, and become extensions of the Sword Dancer's divine devotion. Through practice and faith, a Sword Dancer learns to weave swordsmanship, divine spellcasting, and sacred song into a single flowing performance.

### Level 3: Sword Dancer Domain Spells

Your connection to the Sword Dancer tradition ensures you always have certain spells prepared.

When you reach a Cleric level shown in the Sword Dancer Domain Spells table, you thereafter always have the listed spells prepared. These spells count as Cleric spells for you.

| Cleric Level | Prepared Spells |
|:---:|:---|
| 3rd | *Faerie Fire, Heroism, Magic Weapon, Moonbeam* |
| 5th | *Elemental Weapon, Haste* |
| 7th | *Freedom of Movement, Greater Invisibility* |
| 9th | *Holy Weapon, Steel Wind Strike* |

> ##### Tradition
> The weapon-enhancing spells in this list represent the tradition embodied by the historical **Maiden's Sword** ability without granting the Sword Dancer the ability to permanently create or enhance magic weapons.

### Level 3: Sword Dancer Training

Your sacred training combines swordsmanship with the ritual dances and performances of the Sword Dancer tradition.

You gain proficiency with **two sword weapons of your choice**.

You also gain proficiency in the **Performance** skill. If you already have proficiency in Performance, you instead gain proficiency in **Acrobatics, Persuasion, or Religion**.

For the purposes of this subclass, sword weapons include weapons such as the **Longsword, Shortsword, Scimitar, Rapier, and Greatsword**, as well as other weapons the DM determines are swords.

### Level 3: Moonlights

You learn the **Dancing Lights** cantrip. It counts as a Cleric spell for you and doesn't count against the number of Cleric cantrips you know.

If you already know the Dancing Lights cantrip when you gain this feature, you instead learn one cantrip of your choice from the Cleric spell list.

The lights created by this spell can appear as silvery, pale blue, blue-white, or other moonlit hues appropriate to your faith.

### Level 3: Channel Divinity: Sword Song

You learn to form a sacred bond with a chosen sword and awaken the divine music within it.

You can perform a **1-hour rite** of prayer, song, and meditation while remaining in contact with a sword. At the conclusion of the rite, that weapon becomes your **Singing Sword**.

You can have only one Singing Sword at a time. Performing the rite with another sword ends your previous Sword Song bond.

This bond is separate from Magic Item Attunement and doesn't count against the number of magic items to which you can attune.

#### Awakening the Song

While holding your Singing Sword, you can take a **Bonus Action** and expend one use of your **Channel Divinity** to awaken its sacred song.

Your Sword Song lasts for **1 minute**. It ends early if you have the Incapacitated condition, or you can end it at any time without taking an action.

While Sword Song is active, your Singing Sword audibly sings heroic hymns and grants the following benefits.

#### Sacred Bladework

You can use your **Wisdom modifier instead of Strength or Dexterity** for the attack and damage rolls you make with your Singing Sword.

#### Song of Courage

Your Singing Sword projects an aura in a **10-foot radius**.

You and friendly creatures within the aura have **Advantage on saving throws to avoid or end the Charmed or Frightened conditions**.

A creature must be able to hear the Singing Sword to benefit from the aura. The aura is centered on the Singing Sword and moves with it.

At **17th level**, the radius of your Song of Courage increases to **30 feet**.

### Level 6: Sword Dance

Your mastery of Sword Song allows you to extend your sacred performance to a sword that dances independently through the air.

During the same 1-hour sacred rite used to designate your Singing Sword, you can designate one sword as your **Dancing Sword**. Your Dancing Sword can be your Singing Sword or a different sword.

You can have only one Dancing Sword at a time. Designating a Dancing Sword doesn't count as Magic Item Attunement.

#### Beginning the Dance

You can use Sword Dance only while your **Sword Song is active**.

When you activate Sword Song, you can release your Dancing Sword into the air as part of the **same Bonus Action**. If Sword Song is already active, you can instead release your Dancing Sword as a Bonus Action.

When released, the sword begins to hover. It can immediately fly up to **30 feet** and perform a **Dancing Strike**.

#### Dancing Strike

The Dancing Sword flies up to **30 feet** to a location within 30 feet of you and makes one melee weapon attack against a creature within 5 feet of it.

You use your **Wisdom modifier instead of Strength or Dexterity** for the attack and damage rolls of attacks made by your Dancing Sword.

The attack uses the sword's normal:

- damage dice,
- damage type,
- magical bonuses, and
- applicable magical properties.

A magical property that specifically requires you to hold or wield the weapon doesn't function while the weapon is dancing.

#### Continuing the Dance

After the Dancing Sword has been released, you can use a **Bonus Action** on subsequent turns to command it to fly up to 30 feet and make another Dancing Strike.

The Dancing Sword must remain within **30 feet of you**. It can perform no more than **one Dance on a turn**.

#### Number of Dances

Each time you activate Sword Song, your Dancing Sword can perform a number of **Dances equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum of one)**.

A Dance is expended when the Dancing Sword makes a **Dancing Strike** or uses **Dancing Parry**. Any unused Dances are lost when Sword Song ends.

#### Dancing Parry

Your Dancing Sword can use its supernatural movement to defend those sheltered by your Sword Song.

While Sword Song is active, when **you or a friendly creature within your Song of Courage aura** is hit by an attack, you can use your **Reaction** and expend one of your Dancing Sword's remaining Dances.

To use Dancing Parry, your Dancing Sword must be within your **Song of Courage aura** and must be able to reach a space within 5 feet of the protected creature by flying no more than **30 feet**.

The Dancing Sword flies to interpose itself between the protected creature and the incoming attack. The protected creature gains a **+2 bonus to AC against the triggering attack and until the start of your next turn**, potentially causing the triggering attack to miss.

The Dancing Sword ends this movement within **5 feet of the protected creature**. Using Dancing Parry counts as one of the Dancing Sword's available Dances.

Because Dancing Parry uses your Reaction, you can make a Dancing Strike on your turn and later use Dancing Parry during the same round if you have another Dance available.

#### Singing and Dancing with the Same Sword

If your Singing Sword and Dancing Sword are the **same weapon**, releasing it through Sword Dance doesn't end your Sword Song.

Your Song of Courage aura remains centered on the sword while it dances. Moving the Dancing Sword therefore also moves the center of the aura.

If your Singing Sword and Dancing Sword are different weapons, the Song of Courage aura remains centered on your Singing Sword while the Dancing Sword moves independently.

#### Returning Sword

After your Dancing Sword expends its final available Dance, it returns to you. If its final Dance was **Dancing Parry**, it remains hovering defensively until the start of your next turn before returning.

The sword attempts to return to your hand. If you have no free hand, it falls in your space. If it can't reach you, it flies as close to you as possible and then falls.

The Dancing Sword also ceases to hover if you grasp it, if it is ever more than **30 feet from you**, or if your Sword Song ends.

Sword Dance doesn't require Concentration.

### Level 6: Spellsong

Your sacred song allows you to shape divine magic instinctively, weaving prayer and spellcasting into the rhythm of your dance.

You gain the following benefits while your **Sword Song is active**.

#### Singing Focus

While you hold your Singing Sword, it can function as a **Spellcasting Focus** for your Cleric spells.

You can perform the Somatic components of your Cleric spells with the hand holding your Singing Sword.

#### Improvised Verse

Once during each activation of Sword Song, you can cast a **Cleric spell of 3rd level or lower that you don't have prepared**.

You must expend a spell slot of the appropriate level as normal. The spell is considered prepared for that casting only.

A spell cast using Improvised Verse has an audible **Verbal component performed as song**, even if the spell normally lacks a Verbal component.

All other components of the spell are required normally. Your Singing Sword can serve as your Spellcasting Focus when appropriate.

You can't use Improvised Verse if you are unable to sing.

### Level 17: Greater Spellsong

Your mastery of sacred song allows you to reshape divine magic with extraordinary freedom even in the midst of battle.

While your **Sword Song is active**, your Spellsong improves in the following ways.

#### Greater Improvised Verse

The maximum spell level you can cast using **Improvised Verse** increases to **6th level**.

You can still use Improvised Verse only **once during each activation of Sword Song**. You must expend a spell slot of the appropriate level as normal.

#### Unbroken Spellsong

While Sword Song is active, you have **Advantage on Constitution saving throws you make to maintain Concentration on a Cleric spell**.

### Level 17: Greater Song of Courage

The divine music of your Singing Sword carries across the battlefield.

The radius of your **Song of Courage increases from 10 feet to 30 feet**.

Because Dancing Parry operates within your Song of Courage aura, this increase also greatly expands the area in which your Dancing Sword can move to defend your allies.
